Why these?

We set aside the editorial opinions entirely and kept only the technical reports. What remains is a decade-long pattern: crashes on open, blank screens, articles that won’t load, and a search that barely works — on the one app readers call ‘the only real Jewish-news app.’ A May 2026 review confirms it’s still unresolved.

02 Stability & experience

Three upgrades that sit directly on the reviews.

Each one is anchored in a technical complaint readers repeat — not the editorial. Where to start:

An app that opens, every time

A rebuilt, native shell that launches instantly instead of a blank screen — the single most-repeated complaint across ten years of reviews.

Articles that load, search that works

Fast, reliable article loading with a real search engine (called out by name in a 2026 review), offline saving, and links that respect community filters.

News that reaches readers

Push alerts that fire by topic, a clean read with sane ad placement, and the Coffee Room, photos and live blog woven in.
