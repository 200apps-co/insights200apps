What remains is a decade-long, consistent pattern: people love the Torah content enough to keep coming back - but the playback layer (background audio, resume, stability) fails them. This isn’t about the library. It’s the player the library sits on. And a March 2026 review shows it’s still unresolved.

02 Stability & experience

Three upgrades that sit directly on the reviews.

Each one is anchored in a complaint that repeats in the reviews - not a hunch. Where to start:

A player built for how people learn

Background and locked-screen playback, resume exactly where you stopped, and no stray touch killing the stream. This single fix answers the most-repeated request in the reviews - going back over a decade.

Streaming that just works

Reliable playback across devices with no freezing mid-shiur, offline downloads for the commute (users have asked since 2013), and Chromecast / Apple TV that behaves.

A living app again

Notifications that actually fire, a clean library that surfaces series and scholars, and active maintenance - so the next review isn’t the same 2015 complaint.
